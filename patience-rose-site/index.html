02 / THE STORY

THE PRODIGY — BIO

The Prodigy is a deeply personal body of work by Poshguy, born from pain, growth, purpose, and perseverance. More than just an EP, it is a reflection of a journey shaped by sacrifice, faith, and the lessons that come with becoming who you were meant to be.

Created in honour of his mother and the 10th anniversary of her passing, The Prodigy carries a message of legacy, turning loss into purpose and memories into motivation.

This is not just where I came from.
This is who I became.
This is THE PRODIGY.
